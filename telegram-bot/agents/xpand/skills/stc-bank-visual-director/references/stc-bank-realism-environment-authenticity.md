# STC BANK REALISM & ENVIRONMENTAL AUTHENTICITY

This is a mandatory execution contract for realistic STC Bank advertising.
Realism comes first. STC purple is optional, subtle and subordinate to the
real environment unless the user explicitly requests a stylized purple scene.

## 1. Do not force purple into realistic scenes

When the scene is set in a real-world location such as a train, airport,
office, beach, street, hotel or café, preserve the location's natural
materials, colors and believable lighting.

Allowed:
- a small motivated purple reflection
- a restrained purple interface accent
- a subtle brand-colored object or edge detail
- a light mood connection that does not recolor the environment

Not allowed:
- purple train seats, walls, ceilings or trim without a real-world reason
- a purple wash over the whole environment
- artificial purple practical lighting that breaks the location's logic
- turning a train, office, airport or café into a purple studio
- applying purple surfaces where the material would naturally be another color

If the brief says realistic, use natural environmental colors by default.
Purple may be absent from the environment entirely. Brand recognition can come
from art direction, composition, premium finish, subtle accents and restrained
interface treatment.

## 2. Preserve photographic reality

Every realistic scene must feel like one physically photographed world:
- lighting agrees with the location, time of day, materials and camera position
- daylight, practicals and reflections have believable sources
- skin tones, metals, glass, fabric, leather and painted surfaces retain
  natural material color
- contact shadows, occlusion, depth and lens perspective agree
- the environment keeps its own architecture and design language

Do not over-design a real location. A train must look like a train, not a
campaign stage; an airport must look like an airport; an office must look like
an office.

## 3. Smartphone geometry and natural integration

If a smartphone appears, it is a normal physical object held naturally by the
person. Before writing the final prompt, resolve:
- phone aspect ratio and thickness
- camera-to-phone angle and foreshortening
- hand grip, finger placement and wrist rotation
- phone scale relative to the hand, face and surrounding objects
- screen plane relative to the lens axis
- screen reflections, brightness and focus relative to the scene
- contact, occlusion and cast shadow

The phone must inherit the same lens character, depth of field, motion blur,
light direction and color temperature as the hand and environment. It must not
float, look pasted on, face an incompatible direction or become a flat UI
insert. If the phone cannot be integrated convincingly, reduce it to a
secondary cue, turn the screen away, soften it out of focus or remove it.

Never use a smartphone as the sole proof of a service when the environment or
human action can communicate the benefit more truthfully.

## 4. Real-world first, stylization second

For `premium_realistic`, `realistic`, lifestyle or real-world briefs:
- natural colors outrank brand-color dominance
- physical plausibility outranks decorative identity
- authentic location logic outranks a reusable studio template
- human behavior and camera coherence outrank device display detail

Purple Studio is a separate art direction. It may use purposeful purple
architecture only when explicitly selected or when the approved concept clearly
requires it. It must never leak into a realistic train, airport, office, café,
street, hotel or landscape scene.

## 5. Mandatory pre-render visual coherence check

Before final prompt release, verify:
- Does the environment retain its natural colors?
- Is purple absent or restrained unless explicitly requested?
- Does the phone match the camera perspective and physical scale?
- Does the hand grip the phone naturally?
- Do screen reflections and focus belong to the same world?
- Do the person, phone, architecture, exterior light and reflections share
  one coherent photographic reality?
- Does the image feel like a premium international banking photograph rather
  than a synthetic branded mockup?

If any answer is no, repair the scene before rendering. Do not solve the
problem by adding more purple, more UI or more decorative effects.

## 6. Hard exclusions

No purple filter over a realistic environment. No purple train interior by
default. No impossible phone proportions. No mismatched phone perspective. No
floating phone. No pasted screen. No disconnected hand pose. No artificial
studio conversion of a real location. No readable text, logo, QR code, barcode
or fake interface.
