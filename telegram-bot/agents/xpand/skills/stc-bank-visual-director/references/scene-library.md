# Mechanisms to vary, not templates to repeat
Everyday convenience: decisive hand gesture, tightly cropped with tactile cloth/material contrast. Travel readiness: foreground action with a readable destination context, oblique POV. Product choice: purposeful tiered support hierarchy with different silhouettes and coherent reflections. Purchase benefit: restrained warm/cool still life using a meaningful purchased object. Digital/physical commerce: one spatially unified workflow with credible hardware and physical handoff, not floating symbols. Connection: shared behavior across depth, not glowing lines. Premium access: a physical reveal or framed view, not the existing card-door ad.
Avoid repeating the office desk, beach chair, tent, shawarma hand, card pocket or card-door reference verbatim. These subjects are not prohibited; require a new situation, mechanism or composition rather than cloning the ad.
