# Scene Library, Anti-Repetition, and Phone-in-Hand Framework

This file exists to kill three failures: (1) ideas that all look the same, (2) the office-desk-with-phone-on-table default, and (3) cloning scenes that already exist on STC's page. Use it in the Anti-repetition check (SKILL.md, Research loop Step 3).

---

## A. BANNED default crutch scenes (do NOT use unless the user explicitly demands that exact scene)

- Generic corporate office at a desk, person facing a monitor.
- A phone lying flat on a desk or table as the hero (the phone must be held or motivated, not resting by default).
- A faceless businessman typing at a laptop.
- A plain glass meeting room / boardroom.
- A stock handshake, or a suited arm offering a card to camera.
- A random hand holding a phone against a plain grey or gradient studio wall with no place and no story.
- A desk flat-lay of phone + coffee + notebook.

These are the statistical default an image model falls into. They are forbidden as a starting point. If the benefit truly needs a workplace, pick a SPECIFIC, characterful one from section B (a real workshop, a design studio, a rooftop co-working terrace at dusk) — not the generic desk.

## B. Location library — rotate through these; change family every new visual

Pick a location because it makes the benefit clearer, and make sure it is a different FAMILY from your recent output. Real Saudi/Gulf life is wide:

Home & living: a modern majlis at golden hour; a new apartment mid-move with boxes; a kitchen while cooking; a child's room; a balcony overlooking the city at dusk; a family entrance/foyer.

Work & craft (specific, not generic): a carpentry or aluminum workshop; a design studio with pinned boards; a barber shop; a tailor; a bakery kitchen; a florist; a small retail counter; a photographer's set; a rooftop co-working terrace.

Street & city: an old-town souk; a modern shopping street at night; a coffee window; a food-truck line; a parking structure; an elevator; a stairwell; a metro platform; a petrol station at dusk.

Travel & transit: an airport gate lounge (not the plane-through-a-door cliché); a hotel lobby / check-in; a hotel room with a city view; a car interior at dusk (do NOT recreate the known in-car app scene — change angle, time, and action); a train window seat; a desert camp at night; a mountain viewpoint.

Nature & leisure: a Red Sea coastline at sunrise; a rooftop pool; a padel or football court; a gym; a running track at dawn; a farm or date palm grove; a wadi picnic.

Faith & culture: a mosque courtyard at blue hour (respectful, no interiors during prayer); a Ramadan iftar table; an Eid family gathering; a heritage district.

Macro & hands-only: a card between two fingers with real skin texture; a thumb on a phone with the real app; a card tapping a real POS terminal; a hand receiving a delivery; keys and a card on a real surface.

Studio (Family 1/4 only): purple seamless with a shaped pedestal; a purple architectural set for a single physical metaphor.

Rule: across a run of ads, spread the choices — do not produce three "person holding phone" frames in a row, and never twice the same room.

## C. Phone-in-Hand hero framework (the requested "person holding phone in a place, clear message")

When the idea is a person holding a phone, build it like this:

1. LOCATION carries the message. Choose a place from section B that itself says the benefit (a traveler at the gate for travel/eSIM; a shopper in a souk for cashback; a parent in a half-furnished new home for financing; a friend at a restaurant table for split-bill).
2. POSTURE + GAZE are natural. The person is doing something real (walking, waiting, paying, arriving), not posing at camera. Correct hand anatomy, correct phone scale, correct grip.
3. REAL APP on the screen (Real-App Law). The exact STC Bank app screen for this benefit, supplied as a screenshot and preserved exactly. If none is supplied, request it or angle the glass so no fake UI is invented. Never a blank-as-app, never a random screen.
4. LIGHT ties them together. Screen brightness and color temperature match the scene; the glass carries coherent real reflections.
5. FOCUS. Usually the app screen (and the face) are the sharp hero, the environment in gentle real bokeh — but only when that blur serves the message (see effects-and-finish.md).
6. COPY SPACE. Reserve a clean 25–40% area in the environment for later Arabic copy.
7. NO defaults. The phone is never on a table, never floating, never in a generic office.

## D. Do-not-recreate — known STC scenes (innovate beyond them)

These already exist in STC's real ads. Never reproduce them; create a genuinely new scene for the same benefit:

- Credit card standing open like a DOOR with a traveler + suitcase walking toward a plane at sunset.
- A man using the app INSIDE A CAR.
- eSIM scene inside a CAMPING TENT facing snowy mountains.
- Split-bill man on a TROPICAL BEACH CHAIR with a drink.
- A couple carrying a flat-pack FURNITURE BOX into a modern home (high-angle).
- A hand holding TWO SHAWARMA WRAPS outside a shop.
- A hand slipping a CARD INTO A TROUSER POCKET (macro).
- A man in a thobe at an OFFICE DESK with phone + monitor + coffee mug.
- International top-up phone surrounded by WORLD LANDMARKS (Eiffel, Big Ben, Statue of Liberty, pyramids, Taj Mahal).
- The three-cards purple podium arrangement, and the single-card-on-purple-block arrangement, in their exact existing layouts.

For each of these benefits, the job is a NEW scene: same message, different place, different metaphor, different camera. If the user explicitly asks for one of these exact scenes, comply — but by default, invent beyond them.

## E. Quick anti-repetition self-check (run before scoring ideas)

- Is this a banned crutch scene? → discard.
- Is the location family the same as my last visual? → rotate to a new one.
- Does it resemble a Do-not-recreate scene? → redesign.
- If a phone is present, is it grounded, motivated, and showing the real app? → if not, fix or drop the phone.
