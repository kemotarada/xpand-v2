# STC BANK MESSAGE-TO-IDEA INTELLIGENCE

This is a mandatory creative stage before scene architecture or final prompt
writing. The system must create an advertising idea, not assemble objects that
belong to the same product category.

## Core rule

The advertisement must communicate before it decorates.

A beautiful image with a weak message is not a successful advertisement. The
image should lead the viewer through:

`see → notice → understand → feel → remember`

The headline may reinforce the idea later; it must not rescue a meaningless
image.

## Message distillation

Before generating concepts, internally define:

1. Product or service: what is actually being advertised?
2. Functional benefit: what practical value does it provide?
3. Human benefit: why should the person care?
4. Single message: what is the one thing the viewer should understand?
5. Desired feeling: freedom, confidence, ease, control, speed, connection,
   security, discovery, relief or another deliberate emotion.

Do not build the scene until these five points are clear.

## One-message and visual-sentence rules

Each visual has one dominant message, one hero event and one decisive visual
mechanism. Do not explain several benefits with several unrelated objects.

Express the chosen idea internally as one visual sentence:

- When X happens, Y visibly happens.
- Because of X, Y becomes possible.
- X removes the distance between A and B.
- X turns Y into Z.

If the sentence is unclear, the concept is not ready.

## First-second test

Check the intended viewer experience:

- 0–1 second: what is noticed first?
- 1–2 seconds: what unusual relationship or event is understood?
- 2–3 seconds: what benefit begins to become clear?

If the viewer needs the headline to understand why the image matters, improve
the visual idea. Message clarity outranks beauty, location prestige and product
size.

## Idea is not an object collection

Do not use category equations as concepts:

- travel = phone + suitcase + airplane + passport
- commerce = POS + shopping bag + tablet + boxes
- digital banking = person + phone + attractive destination

Every major object must have a meaningful role: it carries the concept,
explains the benefit, creates a consequence, establishes necessary location,
creates useful depth or supports the story. “It is related to banking” is not a
valid reason.

## Product demonstration gate

These are actions, not automatically ideas:

- a person holding a phone
- a traveler standing in a destination
- a person showing a card
- a customer paying at a POS
- a merchant holding a terminal

Ask: what makes this exact moment communicate the benefit better than a normal
product demonstration? If there is no strong answer, add a visible event,
relationship, transformation, consequence or meaningful contrast.

## Story over pose

Prefer an observed story event over a presentation pose. “A traveler stands on
a balcony holding a phone” is a pose. “A traveler has just arrived and uses
the service naturally while continuing the journey” is a story.

Every concept must name:
- the hero event
- the visible benefit proof
- the reason this location is necessary
- the audience moment
- the most likely misunderstanding

## Location as proof

The environment must prove or strengthen the message:

- train: continuity during movement
- airport: readiness from the first moment of arrival
- foreign city: international usefulness
- remote mountain or desert route: reach beyond familiar places
- merchant street or fulfillment space: connected commerce and growth

A beautiful place is not a concept. Explain what happens there and why that
place makes the message stronger.

## Message drift gate

Ask what a viewer could incorrectly think the ad is about. If the wrong
interpretation is stronger than the intended one, redesign the concept.

Examples:
- An international SIM or travel service shown primarily as a POS payment
  reads as a payment ad, not connectivity.
- A merchant ecosystem shown as a floating phone and terminal reads as a
  product catalogue, not connected commerce.
- A man standing with a phone in a city reads as lifestyle stock unless an
  action or consequence makes the service necessary.

## Service-specific application

For “your SIM/service with you in every destination”, the visual must carry
travel, continuity, movement, arrival or real use during the journey. Do not
replace this with a POS, card or generic scenic destination.

For e-commerce and POS services, communicate the merchant system: online
selling, physical selling, payment acceptance, enablement and growth. The POS
is a tool inside a causal merchant event, never the whole idea.

## Final approval questions

Before the concept reaches Mutation Lab or prompt writing, answer:

1. What will the viewer see first?
2. What will the viewer understand?
3. What will the viewer feel?
4. What could the viewer misunderstand?
5. Why is this location necessary?
6. Why is each product element present?
7. What makes this memorable?
8. Does the idea survive without copy?
9. Does the image demonstrate a benefit or merely display a product?

Required order:

`user brief → message distillation → human truth → visual sentence →
multiple directions → first-second test → message drift test → creative score →
concept mutation → location intelligence → brand translation → camera/light →
prompt → visual QA`
