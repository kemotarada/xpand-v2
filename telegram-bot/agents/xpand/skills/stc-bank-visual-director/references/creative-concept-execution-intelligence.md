# STC BANK — CREATIVE CONCEPT & EXECUTION INTELLIGENCE
# Runtime application of the attached premium advertising quality layer

## Operating principle

Do not decorate the commercial message with a product, a purple wall, a
floating icon or a convenient prop. Build the message into one physical
advertising event. A familiar object is allowed; a familiar arrangement is
not enough.

This document is an execution layer, not a rejection gate. Concept scores,
metaphor checks and originality checks improve ranking and repair. They must
never stop image production merely because a direction is simple, realistic
or imperfect.

## Twelve different advertising grammars

For a serious STC Bank request, the ideation board must deliberately explore
all twelve families below. Produce one direction from each family before
selecting a winner:

1. Premium human moment — trust, confidence or momentum carried by a real
   merchant/customer moment, not a product demonstration.
2. Narrative still life — objects, traces and materials imply a commercial
   story without a generic checkout.
3. Material metaphor — paper, fabric, glass, metal, ceramic, wood, liquid or
   shadow physically behaves like the benefit.
4. Monumental environment — architecture or landscape gives the benefit
   scale and consequence; the product may be small or absent.
5. Threshold / spatial access — a door, opening, passage, depth change or
   compressed space makes access and continuity visible without a portal.
6. Object transformation — one ordinary commerce object changes role through
   believable physical staging.
7. Travel / journey grammar — movement, route, packing, arrival or departure
   only when it expresses the service truth, never as generic travel stock.
8. Authentic Saudi commerce — a specific contemporary Saudi/Gulf context
   with cultural credibility and restrained campaign direction.
9. Camera-led revelation — viewpoint, reflection, occlusion, scale or
   perspective is itself the mechanism that reveals the benefit.
10. Conceptual sculpture — one campaign icon built from relevant forms and
    materials; never a decorative logo or symbol.
11. Quiet luxury — subtraction, silence, precision and one decisive
    relationship carry the message.
12. Consequence-led realism — show what becomes possible after the service
    action without separating action and consequence into unrelated props.

## Diversity contract

The twelve directions must differ in mechanism, hero relationship, environment,
spatial structure, camera family, material language, human presence and
emotional temperature. Do not make twelve versions of “hand + card + POS on a
purple podium”.

Hard distribution rules:

- no more than three visible-hand concepts;
- no more than two concepts with a POS as the obvious hero;
- no more than two indoor retail-counter concepts;
- at least three concepts work without a phone;
- at least two concepts work without a POS;
- at least two concepts are environmental wides;
- at least two concepts are macro/close/object-led;
- at least two concepts use an authentic Saudi/Gulf setting;
- at least two concepts are quiet and nearly still;
- no repeated camera family, object transformation or material metaphor.

If two concepts could be merged into one image prompt without losing meaning,
they are not different enough. Replace the weaker mechanism rather than merely
changing the color, props or lens.

## One-event construction

Each direction must define:

- one commercial truth;
- one dominant hero or event;
- one physical mechanism that makes the truth visible;
- one reason for the environment;
- one camera choice that strengthens the mechanism;
- one natural copy-space zone for later typography.

The image must remain understandable without generated copy, but it does not
need to explain every product detail. A phone, card, POS, parcel, customer or
purple environment is only supporting evidence when the relationship between
elements carries the idea.

## STC visual discipline

Purple is a brand world, not a substitute for an idea. Use deep violet,
graphite, black, restrained green and neutral materials with intentional
contrast. Green is an activation, consequence or success cue, not a random
edge strip. Realistic routes may use natural Saudi/Gulf colors and motivated
purple accents; purple-studio routes must change the spatial grammar for each
concept instead of recoloring the same counter.

Choose camera for meaning: macro, top-down, bird's-eye, low angle, forced
perspective, reflection-led, compressed telephoto, POV, environmental wide or
asymmetric oblique are all valid when justified. Do not default every concept
to an elevated three-quarter product shot.

## Image-only lock

Do not generate logos, brand names, Arabic or English copy, slogans, CTA,
numbers, QR codes, barcodes, readable interfaces, watermarks or decorative
graphic overlays. Create the photographed advertising scene; typography and
approved brand assets are added later.

## Final choice

Select the direction with the strongest combination of message clarity,
memorability, STC ownership, physical credibility and campaign scalability —
not the direction with the most purple, the most objects or the most effects.
Quality review is advisory. Always continue with the strongest available
direction and send it to production when the image engine is available.
