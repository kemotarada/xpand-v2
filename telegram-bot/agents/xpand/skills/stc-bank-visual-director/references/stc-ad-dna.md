# Evidence-based STC visual DNA
Read reference-atlas.json and the pixels for the selected style. Each row distinguishes visible observations from unknown production settings. Do not claim measured camera height, focal length or official hexadecimal brand specifications from a compressed JPEG.
Studio references demonstrate connected purple planes, card/base alignment, selective gloss, restrained metallic highlights and intentional empty upper space. Lifestyle demonstrates pure skin/material colors, strong warm/cool harmony, a clear action and foreground/midground/background hierarchy. Conceptual references demonstrate spatial transformation; exclude their printed branding, floating graphic tiles and landmark collages under the current brief.
The atlas includes all 17 supplied advertising JPEGs; Railway screenshots are operational context, not art references. Existing repository-only images remain intact. No image is an exact product asset merely because it contains a bank card.
