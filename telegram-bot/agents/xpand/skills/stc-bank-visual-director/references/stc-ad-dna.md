# STC Bank Ad DNA — distilled from the real reference set

Purpose: give XPAND concrete, reusable templates so a new idea reaches the exact level of STC's real ads WITHOUT copying any of them. Learn the recipe (angle + theme + light + finish + effect + color ratio) of each family, then build a fresh scene inside that recipe.

Use this file every time (Research → Learn → Compare loop, Step 1). When browsing is available, refresh it against https://www.instagram.com/stcbank_ksa/ ; when it is not, this is the grounding.

---

## FAMILY 1 — Purple studio product-hero (cards / product)

What STC does: a single card (or a tight family of cards) as the hero on a seamless deep-purple studio backdrop or a purple pedestal/geometric block. This is the ONE family where purple dominates.

- Camera: low-angle or three-quarter hero shot; sometimes clean eye-level frontal for a card fan; card sits parallel to its supporting purple plane, edges and vanishing lines aligned.
- Theme: premium, minimal, confident — "the product is the reward."
- Lighting: soft key from one side, a single restrained purple rim/edge light, delicate speculars on the card's metal strip; firm but soft-edged contact shadow; one thin diagonal light streak may cross the far background.
- Finish/effects: controlled semi-gloss; narrow intentional reflection under the card; subtle atmospheric falloff to near-black at the frame edges. Optional very shallow DOF to soften the pedestal. Never wet-floor glare.
- Color ratio (Tier C): 70–85% purple family in backdrop/pedestal (graduated, never flat), 10–20% cool neutral, ≤5% STC-green accent (often the card's green magnetic band). The card body keeps true material color and neutral speculars.
- Copy space: large calm purple field on the upper or side third.
- Build fresh by: changing the pedestal architecture, the card angle, the lighting gesture, and the single green accent placement — never the exact block arrangement of an existing post.

## FAMILY 2 — Real Saudi lifestyle (Tier A, 0% environment purple)

What STC does: a genuine, current, affluent-but-accessible Saudi moment carrying the benefit through a human action. Examples in their real work: a couple moving into a new home, a hand holding food outside a shop, a card slipped into a pocket, a beach moment. Fully natural color, zero scene purple; brand shows only via a real card or the real app.

- Camera: varies by story — high-angle for the home/moving scene, macro/detail for hands and the card-in-pocket, eye-level environmental for food and travel. Choose the angle that best proves the benefit.
- Theme: real life made aspirational — warmth, ease, everyday wins.
- Lighting: motivated daylight or warm practical light; clean skin tones; restrained contrast; clean air and surfaces.
- Finish/effects: natural micro-contrast, subtle filmic roll-off; shallow DOF on hands/macro to signal premium; no purple grade.
- Color ratio (Tier A): 90–100% natural true color; purple only on a real card/app if present; ≤5% incidental green if it's a real STC object.
- Copy space: a clean wall, sky, or floor area designed at capture.
- Build fresh by: choosing a NEW location family from scene-library.md every time; do not reuse the office desk, and do not recreate the known lifestyle scenes listed under Do-not-recreate.

## FAMILY 3 — Phone-with-REAL-app (lifestyle + authentic app screen)

What STC does: a hand holding a phone in a real, motivated place, with the real STC Bank app on the screen (support/help, eSIM destination picker, digital cards store, marketplace, international top-up, split-bill). The place + the real app screen deliver the message together.

- Camera: over-the-shoulder or eye-level onto the phone, or a first-person POV holding the phone into the environment; phone held with correct anatomy and scale.
- Theme: "the whole bank is in your hand, wherever you are."
- Lighting: match screen brightness and color temperature to the scene; the phone glass shows real, coherent reflections.
- Finish/effects: environment often in gentle bokeh so the app screen is the sharp hero; the blur must be real lens falloff, not a flat smear.
- REAL-APP LAW: the screen is a real supplied STC app screenshot, preserved exactly (real Arabic UI, real purple/white tones, real layout). Never invent UI, never fake charts/balances, never a blank-as-app. If no screenshot is supplied, request the exact screen or angle the glass so no fake UI is fabricated.
- Color ratio: Tier A or B depending on time of day; environment natural; purple only inside the real app screen and any real card.
- Build fresh by: changing the LOCATION and the human story, and matching the exact app screen to the exact benefit — not by reusing the tent, the car, or the beach chair.

## FAMILY 4 — Surreal purple hybrid (Tier C / premium, one physical metaphor)

What STC does: a credible premium set with exactly one legible physical metaphor that obeys real optics (their real work includes a card standing open like a doorway). The metaphor explains the benefit with no text.

- Camera: frontal or slight three-quarter so the metaphor reads instantly; strong central hero with designed negative space.
- Theme: the benefit made physical and premium — access, arrival, transformation.
- Lighting: cinematic studio key + purple rim; motivated glow from within the metaphor if physically justified (e.g. daylight through an opening), never a magic beam.
- Finish/effects: real perspective, gravity, occlusion, and shadow on the metaphor; subtle haze for depth is allowed; no particles, no glow lines, no hologram.
- Color ratio (Tier C): purple environment dominant; any real photographic insert (sky, plane, room) keeps true color inside its opening; subject/product true color.
- ONE-METAPHOR LAW: exactly one metaphor. Do-not-recreate the card-as-door-with-suitcase scene — invent a different single metaphor for the same idea.
- Build fresh by: inventing a NEW physical metaphor (not the door, not a globe, not a portal-for-its-own-sake) whose physical form directly explains this specific benefit.

## Cross-family constants (STC's signature, always present)

- Restraint: one hero, generous calm space, nothing decorative.
- Purple discipline: background/light only (except Family 1 backdrops and Family 4 sets); never on skin or natural materials.
- Green: a small punctuation, never an environment.
- Realism: everything looks photographed on a real set with coherent light, shadow, and reflection.
- Text-free key visual: copy is added later by the designer, so 25–40% clean space is designed in-camera.

## How to use this file in the Compare-back step

After locking an idea, name the family it belongs to, then check the draft against that family's Camera / Lighting / Finish / Color-ratio / Restraint lines above. If the draft is flatter, busier, more purple, or less premium than the family standard, fix it before writing the final prompt.
