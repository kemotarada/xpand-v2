# Purple studio: geometry, color and premium finish
The supplied STC Bank campaign card reference is the primary visual authority. Match its saturated deep-violet base, brighter violet/magenta light pool toward the upper-right or rear plane, deep-violet falloff toward the left/lower frame, near-black products with violet rim light and luminous violet platform bevels. Do not average it with a brown fashion reference.

Build two or three planes with distinct values: saturated lit top, midtone violet side, deep-violet recess. Preserve purple saturation in the midtones; shadows may be dark but must not become brown-black or gray-mauve. Use thin bevels and consistent edge thickness, not luminous drawn outlines. Leave the upper quadrant calm for post-production copy without making a dead empty panel.

Perspective: choose the camera and horizon first. World-parallel platform and product edges converge to the SAME vanishing point; vertical objects have vertical axes, horizontal cards lying on a plane inherit its homography. A diagonal must come from object yaw, a ramp or camera roll, and every object needs a real contact edge/back support and attached shadow.

Lighting recipe: broad soft key from above/right or rear/right creating the saturated violet/magenta pool; deep-violet falloff left and lower; weak fill revealing near-black products; controlled violet strip reflection across metal/glass; luminous bevel response on platforms. Maintain separation with local contrast and glossy reflections, not generic neon.

Do not produce a flat purple wall, a low-saturation aubergine room, a gray-mauve wash, a blue-purple shift, a neon nightclub, or a normal desk/counter recolored purple.
